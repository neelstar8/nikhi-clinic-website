Meet your dentist

Dr. Nikhil Gurav

Dr. Nikhil Gurav believes a dental visit shouldn't feel like an ordeal. His approach blends precise, modern technique with plain-language explanations — so you always know what's happening and why, before any treatment begins.

From routine checkups to more involved procedures, every visit at Gurav Dental Care follows the same standard: gentle handling, sterile-first protocols, and zero rush.
